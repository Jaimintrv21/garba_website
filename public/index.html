About the Stream

Shere Garba 2.0 — 40 Tracks, Your Way

Experience 40 hand-curated Navratri tracks — each listener gets their own independent session, starting from the most-loved song at the top. Like your favourites to push them up the queue for future visitors.

Playback is fully personal — pause, skip, seek freely without affecting anyone else. The community shapes the playlist order through likes; your session queue is frozen when you arrive so mid-session re-ranking never interrupts you.
